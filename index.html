Something off a pizza, or an allergy? Write it in the notes on your order. The kitchen reads every slip.

The dough rests for forty-eight hours before anyone stretches it.

Type 1 flour, cold water, sea salt and very little yeast. Slow and cold, so it bakes light.

Ninety seconds.

Beech and oak burn at the back of the oven. The floor sits near 450 °C, so the crust blisters before the centre can dry out.

Then it comes out pulling.

Fior di latte from a dairy in the hills above the lake, sliced the morning it arrives.

Eat it by the water.

A dozen tables on the lakefront, lit by the town across the bay. Inside when it rains, outside every other night. Tables are not booked online: call, or come early.
